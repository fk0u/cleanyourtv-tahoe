Get started in 5 minutes

Pick your device. Updates arrive by themselves after that.

📺 Android TV / TV box

  1. On the TV: Settings → About → press “Build” 7×, then turn on USB debugging.
  2. Install the phone app on an Android phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Open Install, pick your TV, allow debugging on the TV, tap Install to TV.
tahoe-tv.apk

📱 Android phone

  1. Download and install the APK (allow installs from your browser).
  2. Watch live TV, or pair with your TV by scanning its QR code.
tahoe-tv.apk

🍎 macOS 13+

  1. Download the DMG for your Mac and drag Tahoe TV to Applications.
  2. First time: right-click the app → Open (it isn't notarized yet).
Apple Silicon Intel

🪟 Windows 10/11

  1. Download and run the MSI. If SmartScreen warns: More info → Run anyway.
  2. Open Live TV. Channels play in a light Edge window.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no subscription. The code is MIT-licensed open source.

Which TVs work?

Android TV 8.0 or newer, including cheap sticks like the Mi TV Stick (1 GB RAM). Google TV and webOS are on the roadmap.

Do I need root?

No. The phone app installs Tahoe over Wi-Fi using the TV's own debugging switch, and can restore the TV.

Where do the channels come from?

From free, public playlists such as iptv-org. Tahoe hosts nothing and never includes paid or DRM channels. You can add your own playlist too.

Is my country supported?

Indonesia has the most channels today. Every country is coming (#71); meanwhile you can add an iptv-org country playlist as your own playlist.

Is it safe?

Phone ↔ TV control works only on your home network, and each phone needs a one-time pairing code. Security reports go through private GitHub advisories.
