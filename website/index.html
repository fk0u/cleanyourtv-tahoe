Which device are you installing on?

Clear, everyday names — so you never install a phone remote by mistake on your TV (or vice versa).

Android TV Only · Google TV / TV Stick

📺 For Android TV / TV Box (Not for Phones)

  1. Install strictly on your Android TV / Mi TV Stick / Google TV for the full glass TV launcher & Live TV experience.
  2. Requires Android TV (`leanback`) and landscape TV remote navigation — Android phones should use Tahoe TV instead.
  3. Or push it wirelessly from a phone using the Tahoe TV app over Wi-Fi.

📱 For Android Phone & Tablet (Watch + Remote + Setup TV)

  2. Built-in TV Control & Setup wizard: pair via QR and push Tahoe TV Launcher to your TV over Wi-Fi with zero laptop.
  3. Receive real-time announcements, EPG alerts, and one-tap updates.

Which Android APK should I install? Simple rule:

1. TahoeTV-Android-TV.apk (App icon: Tahoe TV Launcher)

Strictly for Android TV / Google TV / TV Box only. Built specifically for TV screens and D-pad remotes (requires Android TV Leanback).

2. TahoeTV-Phone-and-Tablet.apk (App icon: Tahoe TV)

Strictly for Android Phones & Tablets. Watch Live TV on mobile, use it as a TV remote, and install Tahoe TV Launcher onto your TV over Wi-Fi.

Questions

Which APK goes on my TV and which goes on my phone?

Put TahoeTV-Android-TV.apk (app icon: Tahoe TV Launcher) strictly on your Android TV / Google TV / TV Stick. Put TahoeTV-Phone-and-Tablet.apk (app icon: Tahoe TV) on your Android phone or tablet for mobile Live TV, remote control, and 1-tap Wi-Fi installation onto your TV.

Which TVs work?

Android TV 8.0 or newer, including cheap sticks like the Xiaomi Mi TV Stick (1 GB RAM).

How do updates and push notifications work?

Tahoe TV checks releases with SHA-256 integrity verification and connects to tahoe.kousozo.tech for instant push notifications and an interactive update pop-up modal—no Google Play Store required.

Do I need root?

No. The phone remote app pushes Tahoe TV over Wi-Fi using Android TV's debugging option, or you can install directly via USB/downloader.

Is it safe and privacy-friendly?

Device counts use an anonymous random UUID only—no personal data, emails, or account logins are required to watch TV.
