# Code of Conduct

[English](#english) · [Bahasa Indonesia](#bahasa-indonesia)

## English

Tahoe TV is built by people from different countries, languages and backgrounds. We want taking part to be a good
experience for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback on the work (not the person), help newcomers,
and write so people using a translator can follow.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; post sexual or violent content; share someone's private information; or
keep going after being asked to stop.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues, and may block people who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub ([@fk0u](https://github.com/fk0u)) or a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/fk0u/cleanyourtv-tahoe/security/advisories/new) if it's sensitive.
Reports are kept confidential.

Based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Bahasa Indonesia

Tahoe TV dibangun oleh orang dari berbagai negara, bahasa, dan latar belakang. Kami ingin semua orang nyaman ikut
berpartisipasi, apa pun usia, kondisi tubuh, kemampuan, suku, identitas gender, pengalaman, kebangsaan, penampilan,
ras, agama, atau orientasi seksualnya.

**Lakukan:** bersikap ramah dan sabar, berprasangka baik, beri dan terima masukan tentang karyanya (bukan orangnya),
bantu pendatang baru, dan tulis dengan jelas agar mudah dipahami lewat penerjemah.

**Jangan:** melecehkan, menghina, atau merendahkan siapa pun; mengunggah konten seksual atau kekerasan; menyebarkan
data pribadi orang lain; atau terus melakukannya setelah diminta berhenti.

Maintainer boleh menyunting atau menghapus komentar, commit, dan issue, serta memblokir orang yang melanggar aturan ini.
Laporkan masalah secara privat ke maintainer lewat GitHub ([@fk0u](https://github.com/fk0u)) atau lewat
[security advisory privat](https://github.com/fk0u/cleanyourtv-tahoe/security/advisories/new) bila sensitif.
Laporan dijaga kerahasiaannya.

Mengikuti semangat [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
